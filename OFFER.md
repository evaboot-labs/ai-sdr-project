## What we sell

Example: call coaching software that scores every sales call and shows each rep one thing to fix this week.

## Why a new sales leader cares

In their first 90 days, a new sales leader has to learn how the team sells before changing it. We show it from the calls in two weeks.

## Proof we may quote

None yet. Do not mention customers or numbers.

## The ask

A 20-minute call to see their team's first call scores.

## Tone

- Plain, short, peer to peer. First name only.
- Never say: "I hope this finds you well", "quick question", "synergy", "game-changer".
- No flattery about the person's profile.
